# Conversion Patterns (Above-the-fold & beyond)

## Benchmark teardowns
- **Linear**: tight hero, single bold CTA, immediate product shot, dense feature bento.
- **Vercel**: hero video of product, metrics bar, customer logo wall.
- **Stripe**: editorial serif hero, API snippet, industry lanes.
- **Arc**: expressive hero, single statement, massive product art.
- **Railway**: product screenshot as hero, CTA above fold, zero hype copy.
- **Raycast**: downloadable hero, immediate "install" CTA, clean feature cards.

## Above-fold rules
1. One primary CTA. One secondary at most.
2. Product shot visible within 100% viewport height on 1280×800.
3. ≤ 12 words in the main headline.
4. Subhead answers "what does it do" not "who it's for".
5. No ambient carousel, no hero video > 6s loop.
6. Navigation: ≤ 5 items plus CTA. No mega-menu.

## Section sequencing
1. Hero
2. Social proof (logos, metrics, testimonial)
3. Problem / outcome (emotional hook)
4. Features (3–6, not 12)
5. Proof (case study, integration logos)
6. Pricing (if applicable)
7. FAQ (only if actually reduces questions)
8. Final CTA (explicit, verb-led)

## Copy rules
- Verbs not adjectives in CTAs ("Start building" beats "Learn more").
- Outcome framing: "Ship faster" beats "Fast deployments".
- Remove "world-class", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge" on sight.
