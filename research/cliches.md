# Phrase Blocklist

`copy-polisher` rejects these on sight. Extend as retrospectives surface new cliches.

## Hype
- world-class, cutting-edge, revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class, next-generation, industry-leading, state-of-the-art, unparalleled

## Marketing filler
- seamless, robust, leverage, synergy, empower, unlock, supercharge, turbo-charged, streamlined, intuitive (as adjective)

## Vague benefit
- work smarter, do more with less, take your X to the next level, scale with confidence, future-proof

## Soft modal
- helps you, lets you, enables you, allows you — replace with direct verb.

## Weak CTAs
- Learn more, Discover, Find out, Explore → use verb+outcome ("Start shipping", "See pricing", "Talk to engineering").

## Hedges in brand copy
- typically, generally, usually, tends to, may, might, could — commit to a claim or remove.
