# Mathematical Composition for Logos

## Summary
Constructive geometry gives logos a "designed" feel and provides deterministic constraints for a generator.

## Grids
- **Golden grid (φ ≈ 1.618):** 1000 × 1000 viewBox with a 25-unit grid forms 40×40 cells; major guides at φ⁻¹·1000 ≈ 618 and 382.
- **Modular grid:** 8-unit base × 5 = 40 per module. Useful for tech/fintech.
- **Hexagonal packing:** six around one. Useful for network/infra brands.

## Constructive primitives
- **Golden rectangle:** width 1000, height 618. Subdivide recursively.
- **Golden spiral:** 4 quadrants, radii 618, 382, 236, 146.
- **Root-2 rectangle:** ISO paper ratio, appropriate for editorial brands.

## Applied rules
1. Align stroke centerlines to grid, not edges.
2. Circle radii: 500, 309, 191, 118.
3. Diagonal angles chosen from {15°, 22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60°} — avoid arbitrary angles.
4. Spacing between letterforms derived from x-height (cap-height × 0.72).
5. Construction markings shipped in `construction.svg` alongside final — proves geometry.
